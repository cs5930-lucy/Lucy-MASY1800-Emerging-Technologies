Rogers categories describe relative timing in a defined social system. Innovators and early adopters may tolerate uncertainty and undertake early learning; early/late majorities and laggards require evidence of comparative timing to assign confidently. Fast follower is the case's stated posture, not a measured early-majority rank. The present packet cannot place the complete workflow on that distribution.
